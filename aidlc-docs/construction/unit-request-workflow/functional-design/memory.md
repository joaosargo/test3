## Tradeoffs
- 2026-09-10T12:57:27Z — Modelled request approval as an explicit finite state machine (Draft→Submitted→Validated/Rejected→Approved/Rejected) with a transition table, rather than boolean flags. The two-stage, approve/reject-only, no-override workflow maps cleanly onto guarded transitions and makes the invariants (no skipping the lead stage, terminal states are immutable) enforceable and testable in isolation.
- 2026-09-10T12:57:27Z — Chose optimistic concurrency (version/expectedStatus check on transition) over pessimistic locking for concurrent approver actions. Contention is low (one lead, then one HR approver per request) and optimistic checks avoid holding locks across the request; a losing writer gets a STALE_STATE conflict and re-reads.
- 2026-09-10T12:57:27Z — Persisted an append-only transition history in-aggregate to satisfy req-status-tracking and to feed the immutable audit-trail component, rather than only storing current status. Current status is a derived projection of the last transition; history is never mutated.

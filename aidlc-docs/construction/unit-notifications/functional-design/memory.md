## Tradeoffs
- 2026-09-10T13:42:22Z — Modelled notification delivery as an at-least-once, idempotent consumer with a per-(recipient, event) dedupe key, rather than exactly-once. A durable bus (SNS/EventBridge, decided at infrastructure-design) delivers at-least-once; exactly-once across email + a separate in-app store is impractical, so idempotency on the consumer side is the pragmatic correctness guarantee.
- 2026-09-10T13:42:22Z — Split the two delivery channels (email, in-app) behind two independent ports (EmailSenderPort, InAppInboxPort) with per-channel outcome, rather than one combined "notify" call. A transient email-provider outage must not lose the in-app copy, and the two channels have very different durability/retry profiles; independent ports let each fail and retry in isolation (graceful degradation).
- 2026-09-10T13:42:22Z — Treated notification failure as a NON-fatal side effect that never blocks or reverses the source transition (the workflow commit already happened before the event was published). A send failure is retried with backoff and, on exhaustion, dead-lettered for operability — it is never propagated back as a workflow error.
